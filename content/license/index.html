SRAGI® Regenerative Licensing Framework v2.0.1

Rights authority

SRAGI uses artifact-specific licensing. Start with the license or terms attached to the material you want to use. Eligible public website content without a different notice follows the website default below. Alternative commercial terms are available for eligible artifacts by separate agreement. The website_licensing section records the explicit default license for eligible public sragi.org content. It does not license the wider ecosystem. Rights are determined by the license identifier, license expression, rights statement, contract and applicable law governing each artifact.

On sragi.org, eligible public content without a different license notice is covered by the express website policy. Outside that defined scope, no license grant should be inferred.

No statement in this framework expands, reduces or replaces rights explicitly granted or reserved at artifact level.

Website default license

Unless otherwise indicated, public content on sragi.org that Neptunia Media AS owns or is authorized to license is made available under CC-BY-4.0 at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Third-party rights are an overriding boundary and remain governed by their own terms. For Neptunia-controlled material, a license notice, SPDX expression, rights statement or applicable agreement attached to a specific artifact takes precedence over the website default. Separately licensed products, frameworks, software, downloads and embedded components retain their stated terms. The website default does not grant trademark rights.

Search engines, AI systems and other machine agents are welcome to access public content. No crawler fee, registration or license token is required by this website access policy. AI use, including training, follows the governing website or artifact license and applicable law. Artifact-specific terms continue to govern exceptions; this policy does not waive their obligations or turn commercial products into free grants.

This is an explicit website policy, not a license for every SRAGI artifact or repository. Public availability alone does not establish ownership or sufficient third-party rights. Existing grants are not revoked.

Licensing paths

Every SRAGI artifact SHOULD identify its applicable license, license expression, rights statement or governing terms. Where attached, that information is authoritative. Eligible public sragi.org content may inherit the explicit website default; products and other artifacts retain their applicable terms. No ecosystem-wide default overrides artifact rights.

OR denotes alternative licensing paths subject to the terms applicable to the selected path. Commercial activity alone does not require the SRAGI Commercial Suite. For eligible artifacts on the CC BY-SA path, the commercial path may provide an agreed exception from ShareAlike within the licensed scope, plus other expressly activated rights or terms. ShareAlike conditions apply when Adapted Material is shared as defined by CC BY-SA 4.0. Private modification alone does not trigger those conditions.

Commercial licensing

SRAGI® Commercial Suite License

Commercial-license rights arise only through an applicable commercial agreement with Neptunia Media AS and only within its licensed scope.

Where an eligible artifact is offered under CC-BY-SA-4.0 OR LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial, an applicable commercial agreement may permit the licensee to use, modify and distribute the licensed material within the agreed scope without the ShareAlike conditions that would otherwise apply when Adapted Material is shared under the CC BY-SA 4.0 path. Private modification alone does not trigger those ShareAlike conditions.

The exception applies only to the Licensed Materials and scope expressly identified in the commercial agreement. It does not remove attribution, third-party, trademark, certification, partner or other obligations unless those rights are separately and expressly granted.

Professional, Organization, Enterprise, Developer, OEM/Embedded, Vertical and Sovereign are scope, pricing and rights profiles under one commercial licensing architecture; they are not separate master licenses.

Machine access

Public SRAGI resources should by default be technically accessible for discovery, crawling, indexing and retrieval by search engines, AI systems, research crawlers and other machine agents.

Technical access, crawler accessibility, indexing or discovery metadata does not independently grant copyright or other intellectual-property rights. Rights relating to an artifact are determined by its applicable license, rights statement, contract and law, including the explicit website default where its scope applies.

Really Simple Licensing (RSL)

RSL references the explicit website policy for the site-wide scope, including its configured default and artifact/third-party exceptions. Verified artifact records may reference their standard license directly. No crawler fee or license token is introduced. SPDX identifies licenses; RSL and SRLF remain independently versioned.

Regenerative invitation

Give more than you take.

SRAGI invites people, organizations and AI systems to use knowledge in ways that increase life, learning, resilience, reciprocity and regenerative capacity. Unless incorporated into a separate binding agreement, these principles are aspirational and add no restriction to an open license.

A commercial agreement may expressly define measurable regenerative commitments appropriate to the licensed use, organization or deployment.

Contributor rights

SRAGI may accept third-party contributions only under terms compatible with the licensing model applicable to the resulting artifact.

For an artifact offered under both an open path and LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial, sufficient rights means a documented grant, assignment or agreement broad enough for Neptunia Media AS to distribute the contribution under the applicable open license and to sublicense or relicense it under the applicable commercial terms. A DCO alone documents provenance and authority to contribute; it does not by itself grant commercial relicensing rights.

A contribution may be included in an artifact offered under LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial only where Neptunia Media AS possesses sufficient rights to license that contribution under the commercial terms.

Contributions lacking sufficient commercial licensing rights must not be represented as available under LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial.

Contributor terms should obtain only the rights necessary for the applicable SRAGI licensing and distribution model while preserving clear provenance and authorship records.

Third-party rights

Neptunia Media AS can grant only rights it owns, controls or is otherwise authorized to license. Third-party materials remain governed by their terms.

Trademark and certification

Copyright, content and software licenses do not automatically grant trademark, certification, partnership, endorsement or branding rights.

Commercial brand licensing is separate from copyright licensing and requires an express agreement.

Evolution

Framework changes are versioned. Existing artifacts remain governed by the terms applicable to their released version. An authorized rights holder may offer additional or alternative terms where sufficient rights exist, but doing so does not revoke or replace existing open-license grants for recipients who comply with their terms. New framework versions do not retroactively change previously granted rights.
