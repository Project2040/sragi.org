# SRAGI® Contributor Rights Framework

## Purpose

SRAGI accepts contributions only where the rights chain remains clear enough to support the licensing model of the resulting artifact.

This document is a contributor-rights framework and contributor notice. It is **not** the Contributor License Agreement itself.

The current CLA is [`CLA.md`](CLA.md). Where a repository or contribution class requires CLA acceptance, that agreement or another sufficient rights instrument must be completed before merge.

## Core rule

A contribution may be included in an artifact offered under `LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial` only where Neptunia Media AS has sufficient rights to license that contribution under the applicable commercial terms.

Contributions for which sufficient commercial licensing rights have not been obtained must not be represented as available under `LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial`.

## What “sufficient rights” means

For an artifact offered under both an open path and `LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial`, **sufficient rights** means a documented grant, assignment or agreement broad enough for Neptunia Media AS to:

1. distribute the contribution under the applicable open licence; and
2. sublicense or relicense the contribution under the applicable commercial terms.

A Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) can document provenance and authority to contribute, but **a DCO alone does not grant Neptunia commercial relicensing rights**.

Where commercial dual licensing is intended, use the SRAGI® CLA, an assignment, or another rights instrument that expressly supplies the required commercial relicensing rights.

## Provenance

Publishable contributions **must** preserve, as applicable:

- contributor identity or recorded provenance;
- source and origin;
- applicable licence or rights statement;
- third-party notices;
- sufficient evidence of authority to contribute;
- relevant employer, client or other third-party rights information.

## Employer and client rights

Contributors are responsible for ensuring that an employer, client or other third party does not own or restrict rights needed for the intended contribution.

Where employment, commissioned work, client work or another relationship may affect ownership or licensing authority, the contributor must obtain and document the necessary permission before merge.

## Rights discipline

Contributor terms should obtain only the rights necessary for the applicable SRAGI licensing and distribution model while preserving clear provenance and authorship records.

Nothing in this framework transfers ownership merely by submitting an idea, issue, discussion comment or other material unless an applicable contribution process or agreement expressly provides otherwise.

## Commercial relicensing

Where a contribution is intended for an artifact that uses a dual-license expression such as:

`CC-BY-SA-4.0 OR LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial`

Neptunia Media AS must have sufficient rights for **both paths** before representing the contribution as available under both paths.

The SRAGI® CLA v2.1 also contains an open-availability commitment for contributions accepted into and published as part of the public SRAGI Commons. That reciprocity is governed by the CLA itself.

## Third-party material

Contributors must not knowingly submit third-party material on terms incompatible with the target artifact. Third-party rights remain governed by their applicable licenses, agreements and law.

## Contribution instruments

Depending on the target artifact and contribution class, the contribution process may require:

- the SRAGI® Contributor License Agreement (CLA);
- an assignment;
- another express permission or rights grant; and/or
- a DCO for provenance/origin.

These instruments have different functions. A provenance declaration must not be treated as a substitute for commercial relicensing rights.
