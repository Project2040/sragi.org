## 4A. Open-availability commitment

As part of the reciprocal basis for the rights granted under this Agreement, **Neptunia commits that each Contribution accepted into and published as part of a public SRAGI Commons Project will remain available under at least one appropriate open licence** for as long as Neptunia or a successor exercises CLA rights in that Contribution.

For software, the open path should use an open-source licence appropriate to the relevant Project. For documentation, specifications, data or other non-software material, the open path should use an appropriate open-content or open-data licence. The canonical Project licence-scope and release metadata determine the applicable outbound licence for each asset type.

This commitment does **not** require every Neptunia product, hosted service, integration, proprietary extension, certification, trademark right, OEM/embedded package, white-label implementation or derivative work to be open. Neptunia may distribute the same Contribution under separate commercial, proprietary, OEM, embedded, white-label, partner or other dual- or multi-licensing terms.

Temporary private review, security embargo, pre-release development, moderation, rejected Contributions and non-public experimental branches do not violate this section. The commitment attaches when the Contribution is accepted into and published as part of a public SRAGI Commons Project or release.

This commitment survives reorganisation, merger, sale, change of control and transfer of the relevant CLA rights. A transferee or successor receiving CLA rights in a covered Contribution must assume this open-availability commitment for that Contribution.
