Purpose
=======

LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial identifies the alternative commercial licensing
path offered by Neptunia Media AS for eligible SRAGI® artifacts.

Where an artifact carries a license expression such as:

    CC-BY-SA-4.0 OR LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial

the recipient may use the artifact under the applicable open-license terms,
or — where the recipient has a valid applicable commercial agreement with
Neptunia Media AS — under the terms and scope of that commercial agreement.

Commercial activity alone does not imply that a SRAGI® Commercial Suite
License is required. Where an open license permits commercial use, that use
may proceed under the terms of the applicable open license.

Core Commercial Function — ShareAlike Exception
================================================

For eligible artifacts offered under:

    CC-BY-SA-4.0 OR LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial

the primary function of the commercial path is to permit agreed use of the
Licensed Materials within a defined scope without the ShareAlike obligation
that would otherwise apply under the CC BY-SA 4.0 path.

An applicable commercial agreement may therefore permit the licensee to use,
modify, combine and distribute the Licensed Materials within the expressly
licensed scope while keeping permitted adaptations or combined proprietary
materials closed, subject to the agreement.

This exception applies only to the Licensed Materials and scope expressly
identified in the commercial agreement. It does not remove attribution,
third-party, trademark, certification, partner or other obligations unless
those rights are separately and expressly granted.

No Grant by Reference
=====================

The presence of LicenseRef-SRAGI-Commercial in an artifact, repository,
metadata record, SPDX expression or other material does not itself grant any
rights under the SRAGI® Commercial Suite License.

Commercial-license rights arise only through a valid applicable agreement
with Neptunia Media AS and only within the scope expressly granted by that
agreement.

One Commercial Architecture, Configurable Scope
================================================

Professional, Organization, Enterprise, Developer, OEM/Embedded, Vertical and
Sovereign are scope, pricing and rights profiles under the SRAGI® Commercial
Suite architecture. They are not separate master licenses.

Depending on the applicable agreement, commercial licensing may include
rights for internal commercial use, modification and adaptation for own use,
professional services, application or API integration, and private, cloud or
on-premises deployment.

Rights such as customer-facing embedded use, redistribution, sublicensing,
OEM, white-label use, platform or ecosystem distribution, model/provider or
AI-provider integration, trademark use or co-branding require explicit scope
where applicable and are not granted merely by this LicenseRef.

rights to register SRAGI names, domains or trademarks.
